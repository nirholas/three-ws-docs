# Code of conduct

We want this to be a welcoming, useful place for everyone who uses three.ws.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Keep discussion on the docs.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks
- Spam, scams, impersonation of three.ws, or promotion of unrelated projects or tokens
- Publishing someone else's private information
- Sharing secrets, seed phrases or exploit details in public threads

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and block accounts that break these rules. Report a problem to **security@three.ws** (subject: conduct) and it will be handled privately.

This code applies in this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions.
