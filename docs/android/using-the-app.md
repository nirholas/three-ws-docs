# Using the Android app

## Make an avatar

1. Open the app and choose **Selfie to avatar**.
2. Take a frontal photo (two optional side angles sharpen the likeness).
3. In about a minute you have a rigged, animation-ready 3D avatar.

No photo? Choose **Describe it** and write what the character looks like, or **Upload a GLB** you already have.

## See it in your room

**View in AR** places any avatar or model on a real surface through your camera.

## Share into three.ws

three.ws appears in the Android share sheet. Share a photo from any app and it opens the selfie flow with the photo attached. Share a `.glb` file and it opens the upload flow.

## Shortcuts

Long-press the app icon for **Create**, **Discover** and **My agents**.

## Offline

With no connection you get a designed offline screen that reloads itself when the network returns, instead of a browser error page.

## Links open in the app

Once the app's signing key verifies, any `https://three.ws/...` link opens inside the app rather than the browser.

## Agent glance widget

A home screen widget showing your agent's avatar, name and how many moves it made today is part of the next release (v1.1.0). Link your phone from [three.ws/glance](https://three.ws/glance), then add the widget from your launcher's widget picker.
