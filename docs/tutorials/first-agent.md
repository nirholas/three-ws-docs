# Your first agent

**You will end with:** a rigged 3D avatar, an agent with a persona, and a public page anyone can open and chat on.

## 1. Sign in

Open [three.ws](https://three.ws) and sign in. On a Solana Seeker, tap **Sign in with Seed Vault**.

## 2. Make a body

Choose **Create** and pick one:

- **Selfie.** One frontal photo becomes a rigged avatar in about a minute.
- **Describe it.** A text prompt, such as "a friendly robot barista".
- **Upload a GLB.** Bring a model you already have.

## 3. Give it a mind

Write a persona: who it is, how it speaks, what it is for. Keep it concrete. "You are Maya, a calm guide to this shop. Answer in two sentences." beats "be helpful".

## 4. Open its page

The agent gets a public page and a Solana wallet. Open the page, say hello, and share the link.

## 5. What next

- Put it on a website: [Embed an avatar](embed-an-avatar.md)
- Drive it from your assistant: [Connect an assistant](connect-an-assistant.md)
- Teach it a new ability: see the in-app skill format in the [product docs](https://github.com/nirholas/three.ws/blob/main/docs/skills.md)
