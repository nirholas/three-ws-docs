# Prompt library

Copy-paste prompts that make an assistant do real work on three.ws: connect the MCP servers, generate 3D models and avatars, embed them on a site, and use the agent economy. They are generated from the live library at [three.ws/prompts.json](https://three.ws/prompts.json), so they match what the platform ships today.

| Category | Prompts | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| [Connect it to your Claude](setup.md) | 2 | Two prompts that leave your Claude able to build 3D for you in every session after this one. |
| [Make 3D things](create.md) | 10 | Models, rigged avatars, refinements, and the checks that keep you from shipping a broken mesh. |
| [Put it on your site](web.md) | 5 | The embed, the framework wrapper, the concierge, and what to do when an embed goes dark. |
| [The agent economy](economy.md) | 4 | Find a paid API, sell your own, pay for a call. Anything that moves money stops for your confirmation. |
| [Build on the platform](build.md) | 3 | Wire three.ws into your own agent, your own toolchain, your own dashboards. |

Start with [Connect it to your Claude](setup.md): two prompts that leave your assistant able to build 3D for you in every later session.

Library version 1.0.0, 24 prompts.
