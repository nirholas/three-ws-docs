# The companion

A 3D character that lives on your desktop, strolls along the bottom of your screen, and walks over to tell you the things worth interrupting you for. It is not a notification center and not another window to manage.

## What it does

- **Lives on top of everything.** It shows on every workspace, including over full-screen apps, and is click-through everywhere except the character and its bubble. It never eats a click meant for your editor.
- **Wanders.** It walks to a new spot every half minute or so, faces the way it is going, then idles.
- **Delivers in person.** When something clears your bar (a message from a saved contact, a meeting about to start, a one-time code, an agent that decided you need to know), it walks to the middle of the screen, waves, shows the line and says it aloud in that sender's voice, wearing that sender's avatar.
- **Respects your bar.** Threshold, quiet hours, per-contact priority and connected sources are set at [three.ws/companion](https://three.ws/companion).
- **Pauses from the tray**, and can start at login.

## What a delivery looks like

```text
+-------------------------------------------------+
|  SARAH                                          |
|  Sarah says she is downstairs and cannot        |
|  find your door.                                |
|  88/100: saved contact; asks you directly       |
|  [ Open ]  [ Got it ]                           |
+-------------------------------------------------+
```

The score and the reason are shown so you can see why it chose to interrupt you. Everything below your bar stays in a quiet feed, with the reason it stayed quiet.

## Controls

| I want to | Do this |
|---|---|
| Pause the companion | Tray icon, then pause |
| Start at login | Tray icon menu, start at login |
| Change what interrupts me | [three.ws/companion](https://three.ws/companion) |
| Hide it from one conversation | Lower that contact's priority on the companion page |
