# Install on Seeker or Saga

## From the Solana dApp Store (recommended)

1. Open the **dApp Store** on your Seeker or Saga.
2. Search for **three.ws** and tap **Install**.
3. Open the app and sign in with Seed Vault.

Store installs update through the store, so you do not need to do anything to stay current.

## Sideload the APK

If you prefer, install the same build directly: [Install the Android APK](../android/install.md). It is the same package (`ws.three.app`) as the store build. Verify it first: [Verify the APK](../android/verify.md).

## In the browser

Everything the app does also runs at [three.ws/seeker](https://three.ws/seeker).

## Requirements

A Solana Seeker or Saga with Seed Vault. Camera permission is only needed for selfie capture; you can upload a photo instead.
